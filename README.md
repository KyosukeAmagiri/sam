# WSLSA 2027 Degree Pathway Explorer

Plan your Western Sydney University law degree for 2027, session by session.

## [Open the Pathway Explorer →](https://wslsa.github.io/degree-pathway/)

A free tool from the Western Sydney Law Students' Association. It works on your phone, tablet or computer, with nothing to install and no account needed.

## How to use it

1. **Pick your degree** from the Degree menu, then your major or minor if your degree has one. Your recommended pathway appears below, year by year across Autumn and Spring.
2. **Fill in your choices.** Tap any open choice slot to pick a subject. The list only shows subjects that run in that session, and you can filter it by campus.
3. **Set your load and campus.** Switch between Full-time and Part-time, and use Campus highlight to see which subjects don't run at your campus.
4. **Check the advisory notes** above your pathway. They flag prerequisite gaps, subjects whose 2027 sessions aren't published yet, and anything to raise with an advisor.
5. **Save your plan.** Press **Print / Save PDF** for a printable guide, or **PDF Options** to choose the layout first. **Copy Share Link** gives you a link that opens your exact plan on any device.

Want something different from the recommended pathway? Choose **Customise this pathway** to edit it, or **Build my own** to start from scratch.

## Good to know

Your plan saves automatically in your browser, and nothing you enter is sent to WSLSA or anyone else. If you switch devices or clear your browser, open your share link to get your plan back.

The recommended pathways are prepared by WSLSA and are not official Western Sydney University sequences. Always confirm your enrolment, prerequisites and any flagged issues with your Academic Program Advisor, and check the 2027 Handbook for subjects still marked TBC.

Spotted an error? Let WSLSA know through [wslsa.com.au](https://wslsa.com.au/).
